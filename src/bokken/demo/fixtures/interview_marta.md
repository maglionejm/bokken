# Interview — Marta, 41, Alcorcón → Madrid centro (monthly pass, 14 months)

I loved it the first year. The van was my quiet half hour. What changed is
the pickup window. On paper it is plus-minus six minutes; in my street, since
May, it is fifteen. Twice in June the van came early, I was not at the corner
yet, and it left. When you miss the 7:40 there is no 7:50 — the next seat is
9:05, so I missed the standup both times.

The app says "ventana cumplida" in green even when the van was nine minutes
late, because it counts from when the route was re-planned at dawn, not from
what it promised me the night before. That green tick when I am standing in
the cold is what makes me angry, not the delay itself.

I have not cancelled because parking downtown is 210 euros a month. But I
started checking the Renfe schedule again in July. If someone at work says
another shuttle keeps its window, I switch that week. What would keep me: tell
me the truth at 21:00 — if tomorrow's window is going to be wide, say so, and
let me pick the 7:20 van instead. I can plan around honesty. I cannot plan
around a green tick that lies.
