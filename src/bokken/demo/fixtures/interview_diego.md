# Interview — Diego, 29, Getafe → Méndez Álvaro (pay-per-ride, 5 months)

I use it two or three days a week, whenever I have office days. Pay-per-ride
at 3.20 is fine; the pass never made sense for me and honestly the app keeps
pushing it like I commute daily, which I do not. That banner is the only
annoying part of an otherwise clean app.

The 400-metre pickup thing is the real tax. In March my corner was outside my
portal. Now it is behind the market — six minutes walking, uphill. Nobody
told me; the point just moved one Monday after a "route optimization". For a
3.20 ride plus a six-minute walk plus a window that has been stretching, the
Cercanías at 1.85 starts winning three days out of five.

What I actually want is boring: show me tomorrow's pickup point and window
the night before, in the confirmation, not buried two taps deep. When it
moved, tell me why — "two new riders on your street" would make me feel like
the optimization works for me, not against me. And if I ride mornings only,
stop selling me a pass built for ten trips a week. Charge me for what I use
and I will stay; pretend I am someone else and I will drift back to the
train without ever complaining.
